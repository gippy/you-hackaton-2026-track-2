What else the model writes

The model never writes markup. Beyond picking panels, it fills these fields under the same strict schema, and code checks each one.

Evidence highlights

The intake quotes up to 5 phrases of 2 to 8 words, each tagged threat, harmless or people. Code drops any quote that is not word for word in the report. The incident report panel highlights the rest.

library.py · clean_quotes, web/src/report.tsx

Map tokens and markers

Up to 6 markers on the floor plan, each in a sector with a label of at most 5 words. Each shows one of 16 map pictures or a countermeasure icon, else a colored dot. The label shows on hover.

web/src/floorplan.tsx

Panel titles and sizes

Every panel gets a title for this incident in at most 5 words, such as "Who's still in the hall". It also asks for room: hero, standard or compact. The page turns that into columns.

web/src/layout.ts

Quick actions

1 to 3 buttons for the commander's likely next move: seal or unseal a sector, switch countermeasure, mark people out, or call an expert. A harmless station offers at most unseal.

web/src/actions.tsx

Each step builds on the last

The operator's moves

When the operator steps to the next report, the page sends what they did with it. Compose keeps those sectors sealed, says whether the picked countermeasure still fits, and counts only the people still inside. An expert already paged is on the way, so the station says what to hand over instead of calling again.

Running memory

Each scenario step's intake reads what up to 6 earlier steps found, from this station's saved runs. A later report that says "the figures" now gets a name. Its own checks can still clear it, such as a green badge or a normal temperature. In Zombie Rush, misses fell from 3 of 15 to 1.

Three scenarios

Each scenario is 24 steps of raw reports, with an answer key beside it that the model never sees.

Vampires and werewolves at the Loft. The corporeal playbook. The only scenario with a sky.

Zombie Rush

140 people queue at doors open, with grey skin and torn sleeves. Costumes or not? The corporeal playbook.

fixtures/zombies/

Why only Nightfall gets a sky. Vampires fear daylight, so sunset changes which countermeasures work. Its steps get the real sky times and moon phase over San Francisco. A phantom and a zombie rush don't depend on them, so those steps get no sky at all.
