# Venue research: AWS Builder Loft, San Francisco

Shared research for the scenario pitches. Researched on 2026-10-28 with You.com Search and Contents. Each fact is marked **[verified]** with its source, or **[assumed]** when we found nothing or inferred it. Summaries are in our own words.

## The Loft

- **Address.** 525 Market St, 2nd floor, San Francisco, CA 94105. **[verified]** (AWS Builder Loft page)
- **Entrance.** Guests use the "Courtyard Entrance" and walk up stairs to the 2nd floor. **[verified]** (AWS Builder Loft page). A 2025 event page puts those stairs at the building's southwest corner, next to a Chipotle and a water fountain. **[verified, may be dated]** (AICamp event page)
- **Check-in.** Photo ID at a security desk first, then the Loft's registration desk for a badge that stays visible. **[verified]** (AWS Builder Loft page). Check-in is upstairs as you enter. **[verified]** (AWS Community Day site)
- **Rules.** Events only, registration required, no walk-ins, no parking. Banned items include scooters, power banks, large bags and pets. **[verified]** (AWS Builder Loft page)
- **Access.** Free Wi-Fi, wheelchair accessible. **[verified]** (Apple Maps listing). An elevator is therefore likely. **[assumed]**
- **Hours.** Weekdays about 9:00 to 21:00, closed weekends. **[verified, third-party]** (Yelp)
- **Size.** A 10,000 square foot loft opened in 2018, before the Builder Loft rebrand. **[verified, dated]** (GeekWire 2018). Capacity and AV specs are not published. **[assumed]** A previous You.com hackathon there had 402 attendees. **[verified]** (Luma)

## The building

525 Market St, formerly First Market Tower and the Tishman Building, sits at the southwest corner of First and Market. **[verified]** It was finished in 1973 and renovated in 1991. It is 528 feet tall with 39 floors, and the architect was John Carl Warnecke. **[verified]** (Wikipedia; SkyDB says 38 floors). Amazon, including AWS, holds about 40% of the space, and Sephora's North America headquarters is also here. **[verified]** (Wikipedia). The USGS listed it among 39 San Francisco towers with a weld type that may be vulnerable in an earthquake. **[verified]** (Wikipedia). Ground-floor lobby details: **[assumed]**, nothing found.

## Within a few blocks

- **Salesforce Transit Center**, 425 Mission St, one block south. Its rooftop park sits about 70 feet up and runs 1,400 feet, with a glass gondola from Mission and Fremont and a fountain that fires when buses pass below. Jenny Holzer's scrolling LED work hangs in the Grand Hall. The center closed for months in 2018 after cracked beams were found. **[verified]** (Wikipedia, AFAR, TJPA). It replaced the old Transbay Terminal (1939 to 2010). **[verified]** (SFMTA)
- **Transit.** Montgomery St BART and Muni stops at Market and First. **[verified]** (AWS Builder Loft page)
- **Yerba Buena Cove.** The 1848 shoreline ran roughly along Montgomery St, and the cove reached toward Market and First, so the tower likely stands on landfill. **[verified, exact line disputed]** (Big Think, Shelbert, Atlas Obscura). About 40 to 50 Gold Rush ships lie buried under downtown. The *Niantic* was found at Clay and Sansome, and the *Rome* at Market and East. **[verified]**
- **Palace Hotel**, 2 New Montgomery, two blocks away. It opened in 1875, burned after the 1906 earthquake and reopened in 1909. President Harding died there in 1923, and the cause was never settled. **[verified]** (Historic Hotels, Forbes Travel Guide). A Lady in Red and other hotel ghosts are tour lore. **[legend]**
- **Lotta's Fountain**, Market at Kearny and Geary, was the meeting point for survivors of the 1906 earthquake. **[verified]** (Clio). Misty figures at dawn are tour lore. **[legend]**
- **Ghost tours.** Groups with EMF meters walk the Financial District at night. A haunted pub crawl leaves 582 Washington St at 18:30 and 19:00. **[verified]** (sfghosts.com). This is a ready-made harmless explanation for odd readings outside.
- **Other legends nearby:** a reported poltergeist at 555 California (the old Bank of America tower) and the Old Ship Saloon, built over a buried Gold Rush ship. **[legend]** (Thrillist, Hauntbound)
